# Money Education Whitebook

零花钱、消费、储蓄、投资与风险常在不同年龄突然进入家庭讨论，却缺少一条连续路径。Money Education Whitebook 按 3—22 岁的认知与责任变化组织内容，帮助家长、教育者和内容创作者从适龄问题开始，而非每次依赖一条孤立建议。

## 把知识放回日常选择

白皮书覆盖家庭制度、阶段目标、练习、常见教育陷阱与复盘框架。饭桌复盘、金钱透明和失败复盘等方法，把抽象概念转成可讨论的生活动作；可打印模板与四项 KPI 适合用来记录和提问。它们是教学设计和家庭协作工具，不是已被实验验证的教育干预。

## 一份主稿，多种使用方式

Markdown 是持续维护的内容源，可导出 DOCX、两种 HTML 电子书与 SVG 图示。Wiki 版本提供目录、搜索、阅读进度、打印、移动端阅读与脚注审计，因此同一份材料可以进入家庭阅读、课程准备、文章改写或线下讲义，不必在多个格式之间重复维护。

## 当前可核验状态

截至 2026-08-21，仓库公开正文与多格式交付物，但未提供 README、许可证、Pages、CI 或 Release。正文中的统计引用和效果性主张尚未逐条独立核验，使用者应回到原始来源，并根据家庭年龄、文化与财务情况调整内容。

## 它提供框架，不替家庭给答案

这是一份研究型白皮书与出版底稿，不是经实证验证的课程体系，不保证改善财务行为，也不提供个性化投资建议。它的归档价值在于保留一套可继续审阅、改写和发布的年龄递进结构。

# Money Education Whitebook

Allowance, spending, saving, investing, and risk often enter family conversations at different moments, with little continuity between them. Money Education Whitebook organizes those topics along a developmental path from ages 3 to 22. It gives parents, educators, and writers a structured starting point for age-appropriate discussion instead of a collection of disconnected tips.

## Bring financial concepts into everyday decisions

The manuscript covers household practices, stage-specific goals, exercises, common teaching traps, and reflection frameworks. Approaches such as dinner-table reviews, age-appropriate transparency, and reviewing failed decisions turn abstract ideas into conversations a family can actually hold. Printable templates and four proposed KPIs provide prompts for observation and discussion.

These materials are design components for learning and family coordination. They have not been established as validated educational interventions, and the KPIs should not be presented as proof that a child has achieved a financial outcome.

## One maintained source, several publishing contexts

Markdown serves as the editable source. The project also delivers DOCX, two HTML reading formats, and SVG diagrams. The wiki-style edition includes navigation, search, reading progress, printing, responsive layouts, and footnote auditing. That structure allows the same material to support family reading, lesson preparation, article development, or printed handouts without maintaining separate manuscripts.

## Evidence and current status

As of August 21, 2026, the repository exposed the manuscript and its multi-format outputs. It did not provide a README, license, hosted Pages site, CI workflow, or formal release. Statistical references and effectiveness claims inside the manuscript have not been independently checked one by one. Readers should consult primary sources and adapt any activity to the family's age, culture, financial circumstances, and safeguarding needs.

## A reusable framework, not a promised outcome

Money Education Whitebook is a research-oriented publication and production source. It is not an empirically validated curriculum, does not claim to improve financial behavior, and does not provide personalized investment advice. Its lasting value is the maintainable developmental structure: a body of material that can be reviewed, corrected, adapted, and republished as the evidence and audience evolve.
