# 末日知识库

如果文明归零，你会从哪里重新开始？

一个关于文明重建的娱乐与知识探索项目。沿技术依赖，重新理解火、工具、农业与社会。

![2026-09-13 历史海报；图中规模为旧版，当前结构以正文为准。](/work-assets/2026-projects/day0-poster.png)

## 把“文明”当成一棵可以探索的技术树。

这个项目设定了一个极端起点：回到原始时代，一无所有，只有一套知识。乐趣在于追问那些习以为常的事物依赖什么：一个容器、一块金属、一张纸，背后各需要多少知识和协作？

## 点开一个阶段，看看它的前置条件。

下面是依据仓库路线图整理的阅读导览，展示知识之间的关系，不是现实操作教程。

### 认知与识别

先理解身边的物质、数量与环境。识别是后续技术的共同起点。

### 即时生存

从火、水、食物与住所理解最基础的生存问题。

### 工具与容器

工具、绳索、陶器与织物，让储存和生产逐渐成为可能。

### 定居与农业

农业、储粮、灌溉与畜养，把个人生存连接到聚落。

### 材料与动力

从材料工艺读到机械传动，观察技术如何依赖更早的工具。

### 信息与组织

文字、印刷、度量衡与制度，让知识累积并跨代传播。

## 六个部分，容纳两条阅读路线。

可以沿阶段推进，也可以按兴趣穿行。根基层、生存域、生产与技术、组织与文明、背景域、现代域，共同连接科学原理、技术史和社会协作。

### 沿一条技术线读

选动力、材料、知识或计算，看看前一个问题如何成为后一个答案的基础。

### 按一个大问题读

为什么定居需要记录？为什么技术扩散还依赖制度？把工程与社会放在一起理解。

## 从路线图和总目录进入。

仓库由中文 Markdown 文章组成。每篇使用“问题、原理、实现路径、应用、错误与限制、关联、来源与待核验项”的结构，方便按主题继续阅读。

### 阅读方式

先看 00_roadmap.md 找到感兴趣的路径，再从 INDEX.md 进入具体领域。把它当成文明百科与世界观素材库。

## 这是知识实验，不是生存能力认证。

本站将其作为娱乐与知识探索作品展示。阅读不等于实践能力；仓库保留待核验项，医疗与高危工艺有明确边界。涉及现实行动时，需要独立核验和专业指导，不能把故事设定当作安全承诺。

# Day 0 Knowledgebase

If civilization reset, where would you begin?

A playful exploration of rebuilding civilization through the dependencies behind fire, tools, agriculture and society.

![Historical poster from September 13, 2026. Counts shown are from an earlier version; the current structure is described below.](/work-assets/2026-projects/day0-poster.png)

## Explore civilization as a technology tree.

The premise is an extreme reset: return to a primitive world with nothing except a library of knowledge. The fun lies in asking what familiar objects depend on: how much knowledge and cooperation sit behind a vessel, a metal tool or a sheet of paper?

## Open a stage to explore its dependencies.

This reading guide summarizes the repository roadmap. It illustrates dependencies between topics, not real-world operating instructions.

### Recognition

Start with materials, quantities and the environment. Recognition underpins later technologies.

### Immediate survival

Explore the basic questions behind fire, water, food and shelter.

### Tools and vessels

Tools, cordage, pottery and textiles make storage and production possible.

### Settlement and agriculture

Agriculture, storage, irrigation and domestication connect survival to settlement.

### Materials and power

Follow materials into mechanical systems and see how technologies rely on earlier tools.

### Information and society

Writing, printing, measurement and institutions allow knowledge to accumulate across generations.

## Six parts, two ways to read.

Read through the stages or follow a topic. Foundations, survival, production and technology, organization and civilization, background knowledge and modern domains connect science, technical history and cooperation.

### Follow a technical thread

Choose power, materials, knowledge or computing and follow the dependencies.

### Follow a question

Why does settlement need records? How do institutions affect technology diffusion? Explore engineering and society together.

## Begin with the roadmap and index.

The repository contains Chinese Markdown articles organized around the problem, principles, reconstruction path, applications, mistakes, related topics, sources and open verification items.

### Reading approach

Use 00_roadmap.md to choose a thread, then INDEX.md to enter a domain. Read it as a civilization encyclopedia and worldbuilding resource.

## A knowledge experiment, not a survival credential.

This portfolio presents the project as play and knowledge exploration. Reading does not establish practical competence. The repository retains unverified items and explicit boundaries for medicine and hazardous processes. Real-world actions require independent verification and appropriate professional guidance.
