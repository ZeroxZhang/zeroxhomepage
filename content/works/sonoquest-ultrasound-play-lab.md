# SonoQuest Ultrasound Play Lab

很多儿童科普页面只能观看图片或点击答案，孩子很少有机会通过连续操作形成判断。SonoQuest 把屏幕变成一间虚构的超声探索室：目标藏在灰阶噪声中，玩家需要移动探头、改变参数、观察反馈，再决定自己是否找到了线索。

这是一款面向亲子互动的浏览器游戏。它借用“扫描—调节—冻结—记录”的操作节奏，设计了一次可以共同讨论的寻找任务。页面没有真实病例和医学结论，所有回声、目标、数值与报告都由游戏逻辑生成。

## 参数变化会直接改动画面反馈

开始前可以从 10 种虚构目标中自行选择，或随机放入 5 个。目标包括星点细菌、蛔虫与绦虫造型，以及草莓、玉米、饼干等食物线索；位置、角度和尺度每局都会变化。已发现目标保持显影，便于孩子确认进度并继续寻找剩余对象。

扫描时可切换凸阵与线阵探头，使用鼠标、触控板或手指移动扫描区域。总增益影响回声亮度，深度改变刻度与测量换算，动态范围调整对比，雷达半径控制显影范围。Canvas 2D 根据这些参数持续重绘噪声、扫线、距离环与动态目标，使每次调整都能得到可观察的结果。

## 一局游戏留下可分享的结果

典型流程分为目标设置、设备加载、扫描和报告四个阶段。发现线索后可冻结画面并选择两点进行模拟测量；截图按钮把当前 Canvas 保存为 PNG，探头接管模式可锁定指针以获得连续移动体验。结束扫描后，报告页汇总耗时、发现数量与各目标状态，适合作为亲子对话的结尾。

它适合希望用探索任务引导孩子观察因果反馈的家庭，也适合演示网页如何把专业设备的交互节奏转译成游戏。无需账号或 API Key，公开页面在桌面和移动浏览器中即可进入主流程。

## 可访问版本与医疗边界

截至 2026-08-21，公开源码已实现两类探头、10 种目标、随机选择、增益/深度/动态范围/扫描半径、冻结、两点测量、PNG 截图、指针接管和趣味报告。GitHub Pages 自动部署记录成功，在线页面可访问。仓库没有 Release 或公开许可证；现有渲染测试文件检查的是脚手架加载页，未为游戏机制提供完整自动化验证。

SonoQuest 不连接探头或任何医疗设备，不读取人体数据，也不训练超声判读能力。页面中的厘米、频率和“检查报告”均为叙事与玩法元素，不能用于诊断、健康筛查或医学教学考核。儿童若对寄生虫等目标感到不适，家长可改选食物线索并陪同使用。

# SonoQuest Ultrasound Play Lab

Many science activities for children stop at viewing an image or choosing an answer. They offer little room to change a condition, watch the feedback, and revise a guess. SonoQuest turns the browser into a fictional ultrasound room where targets hide inside grayscale noise and the player has to move a probe, adjust controls, and decide when a clue has been found.

It is a family-oriented exploration game built around the rhythm of scanning, tuning, freezing, and recording. The experience is designed to prompt observation and conversation between a child and an adult. It contains no patient cases or medical conclusions; every echo, target, value, and report is generated by game logic.

## Controls with visible consequences

Before a session, players can choose from ten fictional targets or ask the game to select five at random. The set includes stylized bacteria, roundworm and tapeworm shapes, plus food clues such as a strawberry, corn, a cookie, noodles, peas, a carrot, and a gummy bear. Position, angle, and scale vary between sessions. Once a target is confirmed, it remains visible so the child can track progress and search for the rest.

During scanning, the player can switch between convex and linear probe modes and move through the scan area with a mouse, trackpad, or touch input. Gain changes echo brightness. Depth changes the scale and simulated measurement conversion. Dynamic range affects contrast, while the radar radius controls the reveal area. A Canvas 2D renderer continuously redraws noise, sweep lines, distance rings, and animated findings, so each adjustment produces an observable change.

## A complete play loop with a take-away

A session moves through target setup, device loading, scanning, and a final report. After discovering a clue, the player can freeze the image and select two points for a simulated measurement. A capture control downloads the current canvas as a PNG. On supported desktop browsers, pointer-lock mode provides more continuous probe movement.

Ending the scan opens a playful report that summarizes elapsed time, the number of findings, and the status of every selected target. The screenshot and report give families something concrete to discuss after the search. No account or API key is required, and the public build exposes the main flow on desktop and mobile browsers.

SonoQuest fits families looking for an observation game and designers interested in translating the interaction rhythm of specialized equipment into an approachable web experience. It is not designed for clinical users or anyone seeking training in image interpretation.

## Public evidence and the medical boundary

As of August 21, 2026, the public source implemented two probe modes, ten targets, random selection, gain, depth, dynamic range, scan-radius controls, freezing, two-point measurement, PNG capture, pointer lock, and the report flow. The GitHub Pages deployment had completed successfully, and the hosted experience was reachable. The repository did not publish a release or a license. Its existing rendered-HTML tests target a starter loading screen, so they do not provide comprehensive automated evidence for the game mechanics.

SonoQuest does not connect to a probe or medical device, collect body data, or teach diagnostic interpretation. Centimeter values, frequency labels, scan imagery, and the “exam report” are narrative interface elements. They cannot be used for diagnosis, health screening, or medical assessment. Adults may also prefer to select only the food clues when parasite imagery is unsuitable for a child.
